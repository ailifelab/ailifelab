Workshop v12 asset notices

The v12 foreground architecture, terrain, timber structures, vegetation and props
are original procedural geometry created for this project. They are assembled
into an editable Blender source and exported as workshop-v12.glb. Articulated
people, the waterwheel, water, crane load and lamps are animated by Three.js.

The current v12 interchange has no imported third-party model instances.
The VVayToyek pavilion, Kenney log piles and MountainLabz Fluffy Pine used in the
earlier v9 candidate are not included in workshop-v12.glb. Their originals,
permissions and prepared assets remain archived separately in the project.
The earlier MountainLabz permission is custom permissive wording, not CC0.

The supplied design image is a retained visual reference. Generated background
art is a separate website asset, not a baked replacement for foreground geometry.

The local Draco decoder is Copyright Google Inc., licensed under Apache-2.0.
The complete license is shipped at ../decoders/draco/LICENSE.txt.
No remote decoder service is required.
