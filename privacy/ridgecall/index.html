RidgeCall 隐私政策 / Privacy Policy

日期 / Date：2026-10-08。适用版本 / Version：1.0。

发布方：AILifeLab（爱物工造 / AILife Lab）。

联系邮箱：sherardchina@gmail.com
技术支持：爱物工造

本地通信

RidgeCall 无需账号，不设开发者运营的服务器，不含广告、分析或追踪 SDK。开发者不会通过本 App 接收你的对话、照片、视频或设备身份。

与附近设备共享的信息

开启附近连接后，你的昵称和公开设备身份会与附近设备共享，用于发现和身份认证。获准加入的社群成员会收到社群信息，以及你选择发送的语音、文字、照片或无声短实况。设备可能为其他成员中继加密通信。

权限与自主选择

麦克风用于对讲音频；按住讲话时发送语音，不保存录音。拍摄媒体时使用相机。系统照片选择器只向 App 提供你选中的内容。你可在 App 内关闭附近连接，也可在系统设置中撤销麦克风、相机或本地网络权限。

存储与删除

偏好设置保存在本机。身份密钥和已保存的社群凭据存放在钥匙串中。本地重放防护数据库保存数据包标识，用于拒绝重复通信。消息历史使用有容量上限的内存缓存，可在设置中清除。媒体预览和准备可能使用临时文件，App 会在使用后移除。退出社群会清除已保存的社群凭据；卸载 App 后，设备身份仍可能保留在钥匙串中。

接收方与安全

通信使用经过身份认证的加密链路及加密社群消息。接收方可以查看你发送的内容；清除本机历史无法删除对方设备上的副本。批准成员前，请当面核对身份短码。加密无法阻止接收方复制内容。

政策更新

本政策适用于 RidgeCall 1.0，日期为 2026 年 10 月 8 日。数据处理方式发生变化时，将更新 App 内说明及 App Store 隐私信息。

Local communication

RidgeCall has no account, developer-operated server, advertising, analytics or tracking SDK. The developer does not receive your conversations, photos, videos or device identity through this app.

Information shared with nearby devices

When nearby connections are enabled, your nickname and public device identity are shared with nearby devices for discovery and authentication. Approved community members receive community information and the voice, text, photos or short silent clips you choose to send. Devices may relay encrypted traffic for other members.

Permissions and your choices

The microphone is used for intercom audio; voice is sent while you hold to talk and is not saved as a recording. The camera is used when you capture media. The system photo picker gives the app only the items you select. You can turn off nearby connections in the app and revoke microphone, camera or local network access in system Settings.

Storage and deletion

Preferences stay on this device. Identity keys and saved community credentials are stored in Keychain. A local replay-protection database stores packet identifiers to reject repeated traffic. Message history uses a bounded memory cache; clear it in Settings. Media previews and preparation may use temporary files that the app removes after use. Leaving a community clears its saved community credentials; device identity may remain in Keychain after the app is removed.

Recipients and security

Communication uses authenticated encrypted links and encrypted community messages. Recipients can view content you send; clearing your local history cannot delete copies on their devices. Verify identity codes in person before approving a member. Encryption does not prevent a recipient from copying content.

Policy changes

This policy describes RidgeCall 1.0, dated October 8, 2026. Changes to data handling will be reflected in the app and its App Store privacy information.

Publisher: AILifeLab (AILife Lab). For privacy questions, email sherardchina@gmail.com. When you contact support, your email address and information you voluntarily provide are used to handle your request. Please do not send conversations, identity keys or community credentials.
